# Peace of Mind 🌿

A minimalist, mindful productivity and life-tracking web application designed to help users manage their daily tasks, long-term visions, and personal well-being without burnout. Built with modern web technologies and enhanced with Gemini-powered AI features.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. Smart Tasks & Daily Progress
* **Structured Daily Flow:** Organize tasks by category (*Personal, Work, Health, Self-growth, Errands*).
* **Recurring & Carry-Over Logic:** Incomplete daily tasks automatically roll over to future dates to ensure continuous momentum.
* **Forgiving Streak Tracking:** Maintain your focus streak as long as you complete **50% or more** of your daily tasks, preventing burnout.
* **Visual Progress Dashboard:** Interactive weekly activity rings and completion metrics.

### 2. Long-Term Goals & Vision Planning
* Map out your future milestones, categorize them (*Skills, Projects, Wellness, Personal*), and track actionable sub-steps.
* **History & Trash Recovery:** Easily review, restore, or permanently delete completed and past items.

### 3. ✨ Gemini AI Integrations
* **Smart-Fill Assistant:** Type or paste a natural sentence (e.g., *"Build project presentation by 28-08-2026 under Projects"*), and the AI automatically extracts the title, category, date, and time to fill out forms instantly.
* **Voice-to-Task Input:** Speak your intentions directly into the input box using browser speech-to-text integration, which automatically populates and formats your tasks hands-free.
* **AI Subtask Breakdown:** Instantly generate bite-sized, actionable steps for complex goals.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (Responsive UI with custom card designs and smooth layouts)
* **AI Engine:** Google Gemini API (Natural language parsing & smart-fill extraction)
* **Storage:** Local State & Browser Persistence
